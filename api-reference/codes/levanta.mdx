## Connecting to Levanta

Invite adam@launchpointhq.com to your brand on Levanta. Once Launchpoint is accepted, add your brand in the [Levanta settings](https://buyer.launchpoint.nyc/settings#levanta)

## Creating codes

Add a new Levanta Group in the [codes tab](https://buyer.launchpoint.nyc/codes). Enter the ASIN for the product you'd like to promote.

<Info>
  Launchpoint will make a new link for each creator who signs up, and report back to Levanta as `source_id: launchpoint`
  and `source_sub_id: creator_id` . Launchpoint handles creator payouts automatically, and Levanta will pay Launchpoint
  as a single creator.
</Info>

## Updating

You don't need to do anything. Launchpoint will keep track of usage and pay creators accordingly.
